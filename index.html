What we do

Non-invasive leak detection

We use specialist equipment to find the source of a leak accurately the first time, in homes, commercial buildings and underground supply pipes.

Acoustic detection

Sensitive ground microphones pick up the sound of escaping water to pinpoint leaks in pressurised pipes.

Thermal imaging

Infrared cameras reveal temperature changes caused by hot or cold water leaks behind walls and under floors.

Tracer gas testing

A safe hydrogen/nitrogen mix is introduced into the pipework and traced to where it escapes, even through concrete.

Moisture mapping

Moisture meters map damp areas to trace water ingress and show the full extent of any damage.

Pressure testing

We isolate and test each section of your system to confirm whether, and where, it is losing pressure.

Reports for insurers

Clear written reports with photos and findings, ready to support a trace and access insurance claim.
